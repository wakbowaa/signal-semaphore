# Product boundary

Signal Semaphore is a non-custodial cooperative communication game. It holds no deposits and moves no value. The contract is authoritative for line configuration, validator-reviewed clues, guesses, faults, rounds, and final state. The browser only renders that state and submits signed calls.

AI consensus answers two bounded questions: whether a clue leaks the frozen target or violates its channel, and whether a guess is semantically the same specific concept. Round progression and terminal states are deterministic code.
